## 1. BRAND VISION & EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Carfax** is a luxury "Old Money" fashion house that bridges the gap between cinematic storytelling and high-end craftsmanship. By utilizing a "Hero Piece" strategy and leveraging Sri Lanka’s boutique manufacturing capabilities, Carfax delivers LVMH-tier quality at a "Masstige" price point.

**The Mission:** To provide the modern professional with "armor" that is durable, timeless, and narratively significant.

## 2. THE NARRATIVE UNIVERSE: WINTER NOTE

### Character Briefs

*   **Tim (Timothy Garcia):** Arrogant, logical, powerful, lonely, emotionally repressed. He represents the structure and cold inevitability of the brand.
*   **Saraya:** Mysterious, ambitious, charming, deceptive, powerful, tragic. She represents the "Summer's Embrace" that turns into "Autumn's Betrayal."
*   **Tim2 (The Heart):** Passionate, desperate, self-sacrificing, romantic, reckless. He is the internal struggle made manifest.

### Episodic Scripts

#### EPISODE 1 — “Knock Knock?” (Runtime: ~1:15)
*INT. TIM’S ASYLUM (OLD CHURCH) — NIGHT*
Tim stands center, accompanied by Spirit Guardians (Golden Stallions). Saraya enters.
**TIM:** This place is not for visitors. Leave.
**SARAYA:** You’re supposed to be the best, right?
**TIM:** Best is just a word people used before they get hurt.
Tim vanishes, reappearing behind her, holding her wand to her neck.
**SARAYA:** Teach me.

#### EPISODE 2 — “Summer's Embrace” (Runtime: ~1:30)
*INT. TIM’S ASYLUM — DAY*
Tim teaches Saraya magic. The tension is replaced by easy intimacy.
**TIM:** Watch my hands. It’s not about force. It’s about feeling the weave of the world.
Tim kisses Saraya, but a thin layer of frost spreads from her fingers.
**SARAYA:** You are losing.

#### EPISODE 3 — “Autumn's Betrayal” (The Dance)
*INT. TIM’S CHAMBER — NIGHT*
Saraya dances in falling snow. Tim is weak, his skin pale, veins turning jet black.
**SARAYA:** What is happening Tim? You are not well!!
**TIM:** Nothing.
**SARAYA:** You are burning... burning to keep me alive.

#### EPISODE 4 — “Winter Note” (Final Polish)
*SCENE: RUNWAY DUEL*
Tim splits into Tim (Logic) and Tim2 (Heart).
**TIM (LOGIC):** What is the point of loving a girl who cannot love you back?
**TIM2 (HEART):** She doesn't need to. I just keep loving her.
Tim fires the killing spell at Saraya. She dissolves into paper-like notes. The internal struggle ends.
**TIM:** The noise is quieter now.

## 3. THE BUSINESS STRATEGY

### Product Strategy: The Hero Pieces
For the first drop, Carfax focuses on two silhouettes to ensure 1.0 AQL quality:
1.  **The Sovereign Shirt:** 100% High-GSM Cotton Poplin (80/2 yarn), 18 SPI, French seams, Mother of Pearl buttons.
2.  **The Archival Trouser:** Tropical Wool Blend, High-waisted, double-pleated, side adjusters, Horn buttons.

### Market Positioning & Pricing
- **Price Point:** $165 - $195 USD (~55,000 LKR).
- **Positioning:** "The $400 shirt for $180." Direct-to-consumer luxury.

### The "Missing 20%" Defense Layer
1.  **Logistics:** Use courier aggregators in Colombo (Rates <$25 USD).
2.  **Fit Risk:** The **"Virtual Tailor Protocol"**—confirm customer measurements via DM before shipping trousers to eliminate returns.
3.  **Legal:** Register with the Export Development Board (EDB) for legal commercial export.
4.  **Sensory:** Scent the unboxing tissue paper with a signature woody fragrance.

## 4. THE COMIC STORE: MERCHANDISING & SYNERGY

The **Comic Store** serves as the auxiliary arm of the brand, catering to the fantasy elements of the "Winter Note" universe.
- **Product Lines:** Replicas of character wands (Tim/Saraya) and aesthetic jewelry (rings, amulets) featured in the episodes.
- **Role:** Lower price-point entry for fans of the series, creating a separate but synergistic revenue stream while Carfax maintains the high-fashion core.

---

## 5. MANUFACTURING & SOURCING (SRI LANKA)

- **Fabric:** Sourced from Pettah (2nd Cross Street) wholesalers—specifically looking for export-quality cotton/wool deadstock.
- **Labor:** Boutique CMT units in Maharagama or Piliyandala. Demand 18 SPI (Stitches Per Inch) as the mandatory quality threshold.

---

## 6. THE TECHNICAL EDGE: ORIGIN LEDGER

Every garment includes a discrete QR code on the care label:
- **Transparency:** Links to a page showing the fabric origin, the tailor's name, and the specific scene in "Winter Note" where the design appeared.
- **Exclusivity:** Assigns a digital serial number (e.g., "01 of 50") to the physical piece.
