# THE CARFAX FISCAL LEDGER
## *A 12-Month Financial Roadmap & Unit Economic Audit*

---

## 1. STRATEGIC OVERVIEW
The Carfax financial model is built on **High Margin, Low Volume** (HMLV) principles. We prioritize capital efficiency by utilizing deadstock materials and boutique manufacturing, allowing for a "Masstige" pricing strategy that achieves luxury-tier margins with minimal overhead.

## 4. INVENTORY STRATEGY: THE GOLDEN RATIO
To avoid the "Deadstock Trap," all production runs follow a rigid size distribution based on target market data for the Asian/European professional:

| Size | Small | Medium | Large | X-Large | Total |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ratio** | 20% | 40% | 30% | 10% | 100% |
| **Units (100 Run)**| 20 | 40 | 30 | 10 | 100 |

---

## 5. RISK MITIGATION (THE DEFENSE LAYER)

*   **Currency Fluctuation:** All retail pricing is pegged to USD to hedge against LKR volatility.
*   **Return Loss:** "Virtual Tailor" verification reduces the return rate target to <2%. Returns are treated as total inventory losses due to Sri Lankan re-import complexities.
*   **Shipping Friction:** International shipping costs ($20-$25) are passed to the customer or absorbed by the 80% margin depending on promotional cycles.
*   **Capital Buffer:** We maintain a minimum cash reserve of $5,000 after Month 6 to handle unforeseen supply chain disruptions.
