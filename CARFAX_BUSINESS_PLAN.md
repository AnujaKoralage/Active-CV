# Carfax: The Sovereign Launch Protocol

This document serves as the strategic roadmap for the launch of **Carfax**, a Masstige-Luxury "Old Money" clothing brand, synchronized with the **Winter Note** cinematic series.

## 1. The Hero Piece Strategy
To maximize the initial **1.2M LKR (~$4,000 USD)** capital, focus on the "Foundational Duo":

### The Sovereign Shirt
*   **Fabric:** 100% High-GSM Cotton Poplin (80/2 or 100/2 yarn count).
*   **Construction:** 18 Stitches Per Inch (SPI), French seams, English Spread collar.
*   **Narrative:** Tim’s primary garment in Episode 1 & 2.

### The Archival Trouser
*   **Fabric:** Tropical Wool blend or Heavy Cotton Gabardine (Deep Navy/Charcoal).
*   **Details:** High-waisted, double-pleated, side adjusters, no belt loops.
*   **Narrative:** Represents the structural authority of Tim’s character.

---

## 2. Unit Economics & Pricing (Asia Focus)
Targeting the "Masstige" sweet spot to ensure high sell-through while maintaining luxury margins.

*   **Target Landed COGS:** ~$35 - $45 USD (10,500 - 13,500 LKR).
*   **Retail Price:** **$185 USD (~55,000 LKR)**.
*   **Positioning:** "The $400 shirt for $180." Direct-to-consumer luxury without the traditional markups.
*   **Strategy:** Sell 100 units -> Generate ~$18,500 (~5.5M LKR) -> Reinvest in the next capsule.

---

## 3. Manufacturing Sourcing (Sri Lanka)
Avoid 1000+ MOQ factories. Use the **Boutique CMT Protocol**:

1.  **Sourcing:** Pettah (2nd Cross Street) for deadstock export-quality fabrics (Egyptian/Supima Cotton).
2.  **Partners:** Identify small-batch CMT (Cut, Make, Trim) units or Sampling Houses in Maharagama/Piliyandala.
3.  **Vetting:** Demand 18 SPI and French Seams. The sample is the test.
4.  **Quantity:** Aim for 50 shirts and 50 trousers for the first drop.

## 5. The IT Edge: The Origin Ledger
Every Carfax garment includes a QR code on the internal label leading to its **Digital Birth Certificate**:
*   **Provenance:** Source of fabric and name of the artisan.
*   **Authenticity:** Unique serial number (e.g., 04/50).
*   **Lore:** Link to the specific Winter Note episode it appeared in.
