### The Vision: Old Money for a New World
Carfax is designed to redefine the "Old Money" aesthetic for a global audience, starting with a strategic foothold in Sri Lanka. We are capturing the essence of timeless, sophisticated, and understated elegance—the kind that doesn't scream for attention but commands the room.

### Phase 1: The Foundation – The Professional Collection
We are launching with a curated Women’s Office Wear line. This is the uniform of the high-achiever, designed to transition from the boardroom to the members' club.
*   **The Palette:** Strictly curated Black, White, Deep Navy, Cream, Forest Green, and Camel.
*   **The Materials:** **Proprietary Tropical Wool-Silk** and **"Hydro-Silk" 22mm Sand-Washed Silk Crepe**.
*   **Positioning:** **Masstige-Luxury ($180 - $450 USD)**. We occupy the "Contemporary-Luxury Gap," offering bespoke-level construction that challenges the legacy heritage houses of Europe.

### Phase 2: The Evolution – Tailoring and Quiet Luxury Activewear
Our growth trajectory leads us into two distinct yet complementary categories:
1.  **Men’s Tailored Items:** Bringing the "Old Money" aesthetic to the modern man with bespoke-quality tailoring.
2.  **Quiet Luxury Activewear:** High-performance gear reimagined through a bespoke lens. These garments feature a **Hidden Carfax Signature**—a discreet, tonal embroidery and a numbered gold button—signaling membership in a global elite of high-achievers.

### Strategic Roadmap & Logistics
Why Sri Lanka? It is the **Artisan Republic of the New South**. We aren't utilizing mass-manufacturing; we are utilizing **Vertical Integrity**. We own the relationship with our mills and master tailors (Kuweni, Tranzlife), ensuring a "Garments Without Guilt" certified supply chain that offers radical transparency—the true luxury of 2024.

### The Values: Durability as Sustainability
At Carfax, sustainability isn't a marketing buzzword; it is a byproduct of quality. We believe in "fewer, better things." By focusing on construction that lasts, we are building a brand that stands for **longevity**. We aren't selling clothes; we are selling assets.

## Technical & Operational Appendix (Revision 3.0: The Industrialist Fortress)

### 1. Technical Specifications & Innovation
*   **Hydro-Silk Finish:** A proprietary nano-treatment that modified silk fibers to be water-repellent and wrinkle-resistant, ensuring durability for the 14-hour workday.
*   **Construction:** **Half-Canvassed** horsehair tailoring; **French Seams** for all silk items; **Curtain Waistbands** for precision-fit trousers.
*   **Precision:** Minimum 16 Stitches Per Inch (SPI) on structural seams; 18 SPI on finish points.

### 3. Inventory & SKU Management
*   **The 70/30 Model:** 70% of inventory is dedicated to the "Perpetual Collection" (Core Classics in Core Colors) which never expires or discounts. 30% are high-demand, limited capsules.
*   **Pull-Production:** We stock raw materials ("greige") and finish/dye based on real-time pre-order data to minimize unsold sizes and dead stock.
