## 1. THE PRODUCT: THE "SOVEREIGN" SHIRT BLOUSE
The foundation of the Carfax brand. We do not sell "collections"; we sell the definitive garment of the modern professional.

*   **Design:** Wide-collared, architectural shirt blouse. Hidden placket. Adjustable cuffs.
*   **Fabric:** 100% High-GSM Cotton Poplin (80/2 yarn count). Must deliver a sharp, audible "snap" to the touch.
*   **Precision:**
    *   Minimum 18 Stitches Per Inch (SPI).
    *   French Seams throughout (zero raw edges).
    *   Reinforced collar stand for structural longevity.

## 3. THE MARKETING STRATEGY: "THE COUNCIL"
We reject the noise of digital mass-marketing. Carfax is built on **Access** and **Authority**.

### A. The Visual Assets (150,000 LKR)
*   **Goal:** Create the "Mythology."
*   **Execution:** Hire one architectural/fashion photographer. Produce three iconic, high-contrast images of the Sovereign Shirt. These images must signal "LVMH-tier" quality.

### B. The Experience (150,000 LKR)
*   **Goal:** Seed the "Community-Led" word-of-mouth.
*   **Execution:** One private, invite-only boardroom trunk show in Colombo. Invite 15 industry-leading women (Law, Finance, Tech). No "influencers"—only "Architects."

### C. The Digital Storefront (100,000 LKR)
*   **Goal:** Frictionless, premium acquisition.
*   **Execution:** Ultra-minimalist Shopify landing page. Focus on the technical specs (80/2 Poplin, 18 SPI). High-definition zoom on the stitching.

---

## 4. THE OPERATIONAL MANDATES
1.  **Strict Scarcity:** When the 100 units are gone, the collection is "Sold Out." We do not "restock" mid-cycle. We announce the next "Drop."
2.  **Zero Discounting:** Carfax is an asset. Assets do not go on sale. Unsold stock is either archived or gifted to the "Council" liaisons.
3.  **Radical Consistency:** The White of 2024 must be the White of 2025. Verify the dye-lot with the supplier before the first meter is cut.
